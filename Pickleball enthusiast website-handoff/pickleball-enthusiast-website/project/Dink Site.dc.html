Questions

Is Dink free to use?

Yes. Finding courts, joining games, chatting and logging scores cost nothing. You pay the venue for court time, and a small service fee on some bookings.

How do I pay?

GCash, Maya, credit or debit card, or venue credits. One person can book and everyone else pays their share in the app.

What if I cancel?

Each venue sets its own policy — typically full refund 24 hours out, half within 24 hours, none inside 6. The policy shows before you pay.

I have never played. Where do I start?

Filter for beginner open play or a group clinic. Paddles are available at most venues, and the session listing says whether rentals are included.

Do you replace my DUPR rating?

No. Dink uses a simple 2.0–5.0+ skill level for matchmaking and sits alongside established rating systems rather than pretending to replace them.

Which cities are live?

Metro Manila and the Clark–Angeles corridor first, Cebu next. Southeast Asia after that.
